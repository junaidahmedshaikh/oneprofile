# 💼 OneProfile - B2B Sales & Outbound Strategy

## Executive Summary
While Product-Led Growth (PLG) drives individual freelancer signups, outbound B2B sales drives high-margin **Team Accounts ($29 - $99/mo)** and **Physical NFC Card Bulk Sales ($500 - $2,500 per order)**. This playbook outlines cold email sequences, LinkedIn outreach SOPs, and cross-sell engines.

---

## 1. High-Value B2B Target Verticals

1. **Real Estate Brokerages & Agencies** (10–100 agents): Realtors rely heavily on networking; branded NFC cards and central team lead routing are indispensable.
2. **B2B Tech & Creative Agencies** (5–50 consultants): Need sleek portfolio showcases for account executives and founders.
3. **Financial Services & Insurance Advisors**: Require high-trust digital business cards with instant vCard downloads and compliance-friendly links.

## 2. Cold Email Campaign Master Templates

>
> I was checking out [Company]'s website and noticed your team is actively attending upcoming industry events like [Event Name / Industry Event].
>
> Most sales teams lose up to 88% of contacts because traditional paper cards end up lost or in the trash.
>
> We built a 1-tap digital identity platform called **OneProfile** that lets your reps share interactive vCards, book discovery calls on the spot, and route leads directly to your CRM.
>
> I actually created a quick mockup card for [Company] here: **[Link to Mockup Profile]**.
>
> Open to seeing how [Company] can cut card printing costs while capturing 3x more lead contacts at your next event?
>
> Best regards,  
> **[Your Name]**  
> Growth Lead @ OneProfile

## 3. Cold DM Outreach Framework (LinkedIn & X)

### Step 1: Profile Audit & Like
- Like 2 recent posts of the prospect.
- Comment a thoughtful response.

### Step 2: Connection Request with Note
> *"Hi [FirstName], loved your recent post on sales enablement. Would love to connect!"*

### Step 3: Value-First Follow Up (24 hours after connection)
> *"Hey [FirstName], thanks for connecting! We just launched a free tool that generates digital NFC business cards for sales teams. Would love your feedback on it if you have 2 minutes: oneprofile.io"*
