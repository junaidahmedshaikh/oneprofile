# 💳 OneProfile - Paid Acquisition & Performance Marketing Strategy

## Executive Summary
The paid marketing strategy is designed to test and validate paid customer acquisition channels with high ROI. With an initial **$1,500 - $3,000 monthly test budget**, we will focus on high-intent search keywords (Google Search) and visual demonstration video ads (Meta & TikTok), backed by retargeting campaigns to capture high-intent drop-offs.

---

## 1. Channel-by-Channel Campaign Architecture

### 1. Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram)
- **Objective**: Top-of-funnel free trial signups & physical NFC card purchases.
- **Target Audiences**:
  - *Audience A*: Real Estate Agents, Financial Advisors, Mortgage Brokers (Job Titles).
  - *Audience B*: Solopreneurs, Consultants, Freelancers, Business Coaches (Interests: Networking, Personal Branding, Freelancing).
  - *Audience C*: Lookalike 1% of published profile creators.
- **Ad Formats**: Vertical Video Reels (9:16), Carousel Ads showing theme options, Split-screen "Old Paper Card vs New 1-Tap OneProfile".

### 2. Google Search & Display Ads
- **Objective**: High-intent lead acquisition & bottom-of-funnel capture.
- **Ad Groups & Keywords**:
  - *Group 1 (Competitor Conquesting)*: `linktree alternative`, `popl alternative`, `hihello pricing`, `beacons vs linktree`.
  - *Group 2 (High Intent Product)*: `digital business card for realtors`, `nfc business card creator`, `mini website builder for consultants`.
  - *Group 3 (Free Tools)*: `free vcard builder`, `qr code business card generator`.
- **Match Types**: Exact Match (`[digital business card builder]`) and Phrase Match (`"linktree alternative with forms"`).

### 3. LinkedIn B2B Ads
- **Objective**: Acquisition of Team & Enterprise accounts ($29 - $99/mo).
- **Target Audiences**: Head of Sales, VP of Business Development, Sales Operations Manager at companies with 20-250 employees.
- **Ad Format**: Single Image Sponsored Content & Message Ads offering a *Free Corporate Identity Audit & Team Trial*.

### 4. Retargeting Engine (Meta + Google + LinkedIn)
- **Audience**: Anyone who visited `oneprofile.io` or `/signup` in the last 30 days but didn't publish a profile.
- **Messaging Hook**: *"Your profile is 80% ready! Finish in 60 seconds and get 14 days of Pro free."*

## 3. High-Converting Paid Ad Creative Angles

### Angle 1: The "Paper Card Trash" Visual Hook
- **Visual**: Person hands over a sleek paper card. Recipient puts it in pocket, cut to laundry machine or trash can with text overlay: *"90% of business cards end up here."*
- **Transition**: Cut to tapping a custom OneProfile NFC metal card against a smartphone. A stunning dark-mode digital card instantly pops up with vCard, portfolio, and Calendly booking.
- **CTA**: *"Upgrade to OneProfile Free Today – Link in Bio."*

### Angle 2: The "Linktree vs OneProfile" Comparison
- **Visual**: Side-by-side split screen.
  - Left: Generic Linktree with 5 plain buttons. Caption: *"Static Link List ($10/mo)"*.
  - Right: OneProfile mini-website with smooth animations, lead form, and interactive vCard. Caption: *"Full Mini-Website + Lead Engine ($0/mo)"*.
- **CTA**: *"Switch to OneProfile in 3 minutes."*
